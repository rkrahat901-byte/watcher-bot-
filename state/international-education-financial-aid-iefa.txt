IEFA is the premier database of scholarships for international students and information for US and international students wishing to study abroad. At this site, you will find the most comprehensive scholarship and grant listings, plus international student loan programs and other information to promote study abroad. Our mission is to promote international education worldwide through the development and publication of the most comprehensive database of scholarships for international students and International Education Financial Aid information available on the Internet.
