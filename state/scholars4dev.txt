Top 10 Scholarships in Japan for International Students
Japan has become a popular destination for international students, with more than 400,000 now studying in the country. Annual tuition at national universities is around ¥535,800, while private universities average about ¥1.1 million. Living costs average about ¥105,000 a month. Fortunately, there are a number of partial or fully-funded
Japan scholarships for international students
offered by the Japanese government, international organizations and universities.
