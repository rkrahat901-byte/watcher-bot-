Services and information
Emerging Leaders in the Americas Program
Scholarships for students in Latin America and the Caribbean to pursue short-term studies or research in Canada.
Study in Canada Scholarships
Scholarships for students in select countries and territories in Asia, Europe, the Middle East and North Africa, and Sub-Saharan Africa to pursue short-term studies or research in Canada.
Scholarships and Educational Exchanges for Development – Phase 2 (SEED-2)
Scholarships for students from the Member States of the Association of Southeast Asian Nations (ASEAN), Pacific Island Countries and Mongolia to pursue short-term studies or research in Canada.
Canada-China Scholars’ Exchange Program
An official exchange program between Canada and China that promotes mutual understanding. Scholarships are awarded to Chinese scholars for research in Canada.
Canadian International Development Scholarships 2030
Scholarships for professionals and scholars in select countries to study and train in Canada. The program supports the United Nations’ Sustainable Development Goals by providing quality education and training opportunities.
Organization of American States Academic Scholarships Program
Scholarships available to students from other OAS member states to pursue graduate studies or research in Canada.
